Why Note G?

A name with a history.

Note G nods to Ada Lovelace’s 1843 notes on Charles Babbage’s proposed Analytical Engine. In Note G, she published what is widely recognized as the first published algorithm intended for a machine: a method for calculating Bernoulli numbers.

Her work pointed toward a bigger idea: a computing machine could follow symbolic instructions and do more than arithmetic alone. This project carries that spirit forward by making the logic of algorithms visible, approachable, and fun to explore.
